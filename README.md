# agriagent
progressive web app for Filipino farmers/cooperatives that uses multi-agent systems to give real-time crop advice.  
Main Features of AgriAgent PH:
1. Yield Prediction Tool
Farmers enter crop type, farm size, and soil pH
AI predicts harvest amount in tons using machine learning
Shows confidence level and gives soil improvement tips
Visual chart tracks yield trends over time
2. AI Farm Advisor (Multilingual)
Ask farming questions in Tagalog, Bisaya, or English
AI answers using official government agricultural bulletins
Prevents wrong information by checking Department of Agriculture data
Gives planting schedules, crop care tips, and best practices
3. Smart Marketplace & Logistics
Enter harvest amount and desired price
AI automatically finds buyers (NFA, cooperatives, markets)
Checks real-time weather forecasts to plan safe transport
Books delivery through Lazada/Shopee logistics
Shows live updates of price comparisons and weather alerts
One-click booking confirmation with SMS notification
Bottom Line: It's a one-stop farming assistant that predicts harvests, answers questions in local languages, and automatically finds buyers while arranging pickup—all checking weather and prices in real-time to get farmers the best deal.
